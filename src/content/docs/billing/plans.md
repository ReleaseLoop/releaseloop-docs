ReleaseLoop is a lifetime purchase. A seat is **$49**, paid once. You choose how many seats the workspace needs, and each seat unlocks the full plan. New workspaces do not get a monthly bill.

Every new workspace starts with **14 days of full access**. Sign-up asks for your name, email, and password. It does not ask for a card. The trial starts when the workspace is created. During those 14 days the workspace has the full plan, including unlimited storage. When the trial ends, the workspace owner buys lifetime seats from the **Billing** page. After that purchase, storage is 1 GB per seat. [Storage packs](#storage-packs) add more on top of that.

## What a lifetime seat includes

| | Lifetime access |
|---|---|
| **Price** | $49 per seat, one time |
| **Seats per checkout** | Up to 50 |
| **Releases** | Unlimited |
| **Artists** | Unlimited |
| **Contacts** | Unlimited |
| **EPKs** | Unlimited |
| **Storage** | 1 GB per seat |

The Billing page multiplies the seat price by the number you choose. Three seats are $147. One seat is one team member.

The seat panel lists what the purchase unlocks:

- Unlimited releases, artists, and contacts
- Unlimited EPKs and artist statements
- Royalty imports and payout statements
- Release templates and Google Drive
- Marketing planner and budget tracking
- 1 GB storage included per seat

The full plan also includes task checklists, the calendar, activity history, and custom statement branding.

## Buying seats

On **Billing**, a workspace that does not already have lifetime seats sees **Lifetime access: $49 per seat**. The picker starts at the current team size, and **Get lifetime access** opens Stripe checkout. See [Managing Your Billing](/billing/subscription/).

You can buy more seats later from **Add seats**. Each checkout can include up to 50 seats.

If the workspace still has a monthly subscription, the first lifetime seat purchase cancels that Stripe subscription as part of switching the workspace to lifetime access.

## Storage packs

A storage pack is **10 GB** for **$19**, paid once. Purchased storage is added on top of the 1 GB included with each lifetime seat. On an older tiered plan it is added on top of that plan's included storage.

On **Billing** the card is titled **Add storage**, with the badge **One-time payment**. The description reads "Each pack adds 10GB for $19.00." The card shows **Pack size**, **Price per pack**, and **Total**. The quantity field is labeled **Packs**. One checkout can include up to 50 packs, and the total is $19 times that number. The button is **Buy storage**.

Only the workspace owner can buy a pack. **Buy storage** is enabled when the workspace has active lifetime access. A trial, a locked workspace, or a monthly subscription still shows the card. **Buy storage** is disabled, and the note says "Storage packs are available once this workspace has active lifetime access."

When a purchase is allowed, Billing also says how much the chosen packs add, and how much storage is already included with the plan. One pack on a 2-seat workspace reads "Adds 10GB to this workspace. 2GB is already included with the plan."

See [Managing Your Billing](/billing/subscription/#adding-storage) for the steps.

## Older tiered lifetime plans

Workspaces that already own Solo Lifetime, Label Lifetime, or Agency / Pro Lifetime keep the plan they bought.
