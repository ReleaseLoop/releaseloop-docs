This guide gets you from sign-up to your first release. A new workspace includes 14 days of full access, and sign-up does not ask for a card.

## 1. Create your account

Sign up at [app.releaseloop.com](https://app.releaseloop.com/auth?mode=signup) with your name, email, and password. ReleaseLoop emails a verification link and tells you that you can keep going and verify whenever you are ready. You then go through onboarding.

## 2. Set up your workspace

During onboarding:

1. **Name your workspace.** The hint says this is typically your label name or brand name.
2. **Select your role.** The options are Record Label, Artist Manager, Artist / Self-releasing, Distributor, PR / Marketing, and Other.
3. **Add your first artist**, or skip. Search Spotify as you type. Results show follower counts. Selecting a result saves the profile photo, follower count, and popularity score. You can also create the artist manually.
4. **Create your first release**, or skip. This step asks for a title, shows the artist you just added, and asks for a release date. It applies the default task template. It does not ask for a release type.

The last step is **You're all set!** From there you can open the release, the dashboard, or Settings to invite your team. The full sequence is in [Onboarding Walkthrough](/getting-started/onboarding/).

## 3. Build out your release

Once your release is created, the release detail page is your command center. Use the tabs to fill in everything you need before your distributor deadline:

![Release detail page showing tabs for Tracks, Tasks, Assets, Discussion, Financials, Marketing, and Activity](../../../assets/screenshots/release-detail.png)

| Tab | What it does |
|-----|-------------|
| **Tracks** | Add each song with its ISRC code and artist credits -- essential for matching royalties later |
| **Tasks** | Build your release checklist: submit to distributor, pitch to Spotify editorial, send press servicing, schedule social content, set up pre-save link |
| **Financials** | Set a release budget and track expenses like mixing, mastering, artwork, and PR costs |
| **Marketing** | Plan your rollout -- playlist pitches, Instagram Reels, TikTok teasers, press outreach, and anything else in your campaign |
| **Assets** | Upload cover artwork, press photos, and stems, or connect Google Drive for your existing folders |
| **Discussion** | Discuss the release with your team. The tab is labeled Discussion. |

## 4. Invite your team

The workspace owner goes to **Settings**, then **Team**, and clicks **Invite Member**. Assign a role and choose entire-workspace or specific-artist access. See [Team Members & Roles](/team/members/).

## 5. Explore more features

- Use **Templates** to save your go-to release checklist and reuse it every time you set up a new single or album campaign
- Check the **Calendar** for a timeline view of all your upcoming releases -- helpful when you're spacing out drops across your roster
- Import **Royalty CSVs** from your distributor (DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, or others) to track revenue by release and see what you owe your artists
- Build **EPKs** as polished public profiles you can send to promoters, booking agents, and press

The onboarding steps are also written up in [Onboarding Walkthrough](/getting-started/onboarding/).
